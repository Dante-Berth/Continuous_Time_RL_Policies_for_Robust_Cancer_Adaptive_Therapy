- **M1c: under medians, M1b's 3-year reversal holds only where resistance is nearly free** — high turnover, 50% missed, median-U gain of the best threshold over MTD: +34 [+4, +58] and +62 [+37, +90] for r̂_R ≥ 0.75, −138 [−166, −113] and −29 [−48, −2] below. - **M1b: M1's cost effect survives a 3-year horizon (high-cost − no-cost bin at 50% missed: −82.5 [−96.6, −68.5], −208.9 [−255.3, −162.3]), but the absolute picture is horizon-bound** — at 3 years, high turnover, sparing thresholds beat MTD even at 60% missed (p\* > 0.6; +92 to +301 d at 50%). For the median patient the reversal holds only where r̂_R ≥ 0.75 ([L968](RESEARCH_LOG.md#L968)) → [L932](RESEARCH_LOG.md#L932)
- **M1: with reliable monitoring feedback beats MTD in every cost × turnover bin (+13 to +123 d); the missed-visit rate at which it stops (p\*) rises with weaker cost of resistance (0.10 → 0.26 at low turnover) and with turnover (0.22 → 0.50)** — H_cost passes (high-cost − no-cost bin at 50% missed: −25.5 [−31.2, −20.0], −76.6 [−88.6, −64.7]); full dose at missed visits rescues feedback only where resistance is cheap (pooled +38.5 [+36.1, +41.0]).
