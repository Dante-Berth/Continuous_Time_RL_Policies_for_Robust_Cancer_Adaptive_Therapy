# Turnover Modulates the Need for a Cost of Resistance in Adaptive Therapy

> **Provenance: `1°`.** Read from `literature/pdf/pmc-8455086.pdf`, the PMC
> author manuscript (29 pp., available in PMC 2021-09-21). Locators are the
> manuscript's page numbers, not the journal's (1135-1147).

## Screen (§1)

| # | Field | Value |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Object | `model`: a two-population ODE analysed under two fixed schedules; no learned policy (p.4-5) |
| S2 | Model family | `lotka-volterra`, S/R competition, shared K, one turnover d_T (Eqs. 1-2, p.4) |
| S3 | Observability | `n/a`: the schedules are rules; AT50 reads total burden N(t) only (p.5) |

## What it does

dS/dt = r_S(1 − (S+R)/K)(1 − (2d_D/D_Max)D(t))S − d_T S and
dR/dt = r_R(1 − (R+S)/K)R − d_T R (Eqs. 1-2, p.4). The drug kills dividing
sensitive cells only (Norton-Simon); the factor 2 counts mother and daughter
(p.5). The cost of resistance acts on r_R alone in the main text (p.5).
Schedules: continuous therapy at MTD, and AT50, which withdraws at a 50% burden
decrease and restarts at N_0 (p.5). Time is scaled by r_S and density by K (p.5).

## What transfers

- **Table 1 (p.29):** r_S = 0.027 d⁻¹ (from [10]); r̂_R = r_R/r_S 0.5-1.0
  (upper limit: no cost); d̂_T = d_T/r_S 0.0-0.5 (upper limit: Section S6);
  d̂_D = 2d_D = 1.5 (from [28]); n_0 = N_0/K 0.1-0.75; f_R = R_0/N_0 0.001-0.1.
  K is scale only. These are **sweep ranges**, with no sampling distribution given.
- **AT gains without a cost** (d̂_T = 0): 3-104 d at f_R = 1%, 19-211 d at
  f_R = 0.1%, none at 10% (p.6-7; Fig. 3B, p.22). A meaningful gain needs a
  dense tumour, e.g. 75% of K (p.7).
- **Turnover:** above (1 − 1.2 n_0)·r̂_R the tumour is controllable indefinitely
  under both schedules, so no TTP exists (p.9). At d̂_T = 25%, Tumours 3\* and 4\*
  become indefinitely controllable above a 30% cost (Fig. 4F caption, p.25).
- **Patient fits (Bruchovsky IADT, 67 patients):** free parameters r̂_R, d̂_T,
  n_0, f_R, bounded [0,1], [0,1], [0.1,1], [1e-5, 0.25]; 15 patients excluded
  for poor fit (p.6). Fitted cost and turnover are strongly NEGATIVELY correlated (Pearson
  0.77, p.10). The sum cost + turnover (cost = 1 − r̂_R) separates relapse from no relapse (Mann-Whitney U = 82,
  p < 1%, p.10). The fitted patients span cost ~0-100% and turnover ~0-70%,
  read off a scatter plot (Fig. 5C, p.26), with high cost paired with low turnover.

## What does not

- **Progression rule.** 20% above the pre-treatment baseline (p.6); ours is
  20% above the running nadir with 28-d confirmation. TTP values are not comparable.
- **No sampling distribution.** The ranges are swept, not sampled; our LHS
  (log-uniform f_R) is our own choice (PREREG S1, risk 3).
- **PK.** Drug dose enters linearly (Norton-Simon, p.5); we pass it through
  one-compartment PK with an Emax effect.
- **Fitted-parameter correlation.** The 0.77 cost-turnover correlation (p.10)
  may partly be a likelihood ridge, not biology. Our Phase 0 found θ weakly
  identifiable from burden (L48); the paper reads the ridge as biology.

## Contact with our design

- **`sample_cohort(..., ranges="strobl2021")`** now rests on a 1° source: every
  value matches Table 1 (p.29). S1 ([L766](../../adaptive_therapy/RESEARCH_LOG.md#L766))
  used exactly these ranges.
- **The provisional regime** (median r_R/r_S 0.30, cost ~70%) lies outside the
  theory sweep (only 9.8% inside, [L703](../../adaptive_therapy/RESEARCH_LOG.md#L703))
  but inside the fitted-patient cloud, where high cost pairs with low turnover
  (Fig. 5C, p.26). The paper can call it a high-cost regime seen in fitted
  patients, not an unrealistic one. The figure read is approximate.
- **The monitoring question** (proposed M1): the cost-turnover axes this paper
  shows decide AT's benefit are the natural axes along which to map when
  missed visits make feedback stop paying.
